MercadoBolt is a marketplace concept designed to make buying and selling feel safer, calmer, and more human, with verification, structured listings, and escrow-first payments.
{:.lead}

Marketplaces often break down when trust is fragile: scams, unclear expectations, messy payments, and no clear path to resolution. MercadoBolt explores a more protective structure, where transparency and verification are part of the experience from the start.

- Table of Contents
{:toc .large-only}

## My Role
- Product concept + UX design (core flows and interaction decisions)
- Mobile-first UI design and feature planning
- Payments + escrow flow design (clear states, edge cases, and trust signals)

## Technologies Used
`Flutter` | `Microservices` | `Payments` | `Escrow Workflows` | `KYC / Verification Concepts`

## Core Flows
- Browse and search by category (services + goods)
- Create and manage listings (with structured fields for clarity)
- Vendor verification (KYC concepts to reduce scams)
- Checkout and escrow payment flow (protect buyer and seller)
- Dispute / resolution concept (clear expectations, transparent steps)

## Experience Principles
- **Trust-first UX:** show the right proof at the right time (verification, expectations, transparency)
- **Clarity over clutter:** structured listings and predictable steps
- **Calm by default:** fewer surprises, clearer states, kinder messaging
