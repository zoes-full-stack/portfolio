  </header>
</div>

TaskBook turns messy scheduling into something calm, predictable, and easy to trust, syncing jobs to calendars and helping customers confirm appointments without back-and-forth.
{:.lead}

Operational scheduling is often split across tools and people: a job lives in a CRM, an installer lives in their calendar, and the customer lives in uncertainty. TaskBook connects these worlds and reduces manual coordination.

- Table of Contents
{:toc .large-only}

## My Role
- Designed the experience: user flows, key screens, and interaction states
- Built and shipped UI (mobile-first)
- Supported microservice and integration planning for automation + messaging

## Technologies Used
`Flutter` | `Google Cloud Functions` | `Microservices` | `Salesforce` | `Google Calendar API` | `Twilio`

## What it does
- Pulls installation jobs from Salesforce
- Syncs job scheduling into installers’ calendars
- Sends appointment reminders and confirmations via messaging
- Provides analytics / KPIs for operational visibility

## Experience Principles
- **Clarity:** reduce uncertainty for customers and installers
- **Automation with empathy:** reminders that feel helpful, not spammy
- **High-trust states:** clear status, timing, and next steps
